## What completion means here

The agreed batch completes these selected finite mathematical additions and preserves the already checked A12 distinctions. The precise declarations and their assumptions are in the three claim ledgers; final checked inventories, classifications, signatures and imported axioms are in `results.json`. Definitions and shared proof components are included in that inventory rather than promoted to independent discoveries.

Full execution coverage of each entire source card would additionally require implementation choices or other fixtures. Examples include an actual serializer and storage service, all declared binding fields, finite-fiber enumeration, and a selected scheduler/fairness model. T61's finite pairgraph/pass-invariant fixture is a separate candidate in `OTHER-CANDIDATES.md`, not discharged by the small replay example.

## Consequences for the source

The checks make existing conditions more explicit: selected questions can survive a lossy representation; exact work recovery has a stronger requirement; retained traces require more than matching tag shape; and a progress bound depends on a rank that administration does not replenish. These are useful bookkeeping and implementation requirements already supported by the supplied v2.3 cards. No contradiction in PAL's spine is established by these bounded results.

O04, O25 and D-FIRST-OCCURRENCE remain OPEN. Multi-parent account semantics are not adopted. Earlier PAL, CHARTER and BRIDGE receipts keep their original scope and result labels.
