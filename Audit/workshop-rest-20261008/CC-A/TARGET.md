# CC-A frozen target

CC v0.2 Definition 5, Lemma 1, Theorem 1 and Corollary 1; P0043-P0050, P0080-P0090, P0103-P0121.

Let j:X->Z and r:X->Y be topological embeddings with dense open image, Y,Z compact Hausdorff, and k:Y->Z continuous agreeing on X. Source assumes k is surjective. Prove an interior fiber cannot contain a point of the resolved boundary; then prove k maps complement(range r) onto complement(range j). Do not assume boundary preservation in the data.

Boundary restriction is continuous. Openness of the resolved interior gives compact boundary, and the receiving boundary is Hausdorff. Derive quotient status from continuous surjection, then existence and uniqueness of the continuous decoder for any continuous answer constant on its fibers. No decoder-continuity field is assumed.

Track used hypotheses separately. Some intermediate lemmas can be stronger than the source by dropping unused compactness/openness. All set-theoretic lifting/fiber proofs are supporting derivations; reuse does not count as independent corroboration of APCI. No general computability or smoothness result.
