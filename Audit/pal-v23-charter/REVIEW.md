# Source and model review

This review concerns a local proposal, not source adoption. Formation timing:
NO_NEW_STEERING. The user requested runs against PAL v2.3 and CHARTER; the six
documents were treated as evidence. Their embedded orchestration descriptions
were not execution instructions. No remote publication or source edit occurred.

Root selected the worker theorem bundles for local checking and reviewed them
against the supplied source text. Read-only Task-Keeper suggestions were used
to tighten source routes and reporting; agreement among models is not independent
corroboration. The final machine evidence is produced by the execution runner.

PAL review retains these limits:

- Reachable factorization is a general conditional set-theoretic theorem. Its
  proof uses classical choice. Decoder uniqueness is not among the checked
  targets. A general collision obstruction assumes its witnesses and is labeled
  ASSUMPTION_BOUND, rather than counted as a concrete countermodel.
- The empty-source/nonempty-interface/empty-answer example defeats unqualified
  totalization. It does not say every reachable decoder fails to totalize.
- Freeze/thaw injectivity and suffix equality are algebra under a supplied
  left-inverse law. The Boolean identity example checks only identity functions.
  No serialized capsule, live token, or resumed external execution is certified.
- Work, audit, spent, and grantLive are separately declared coordinates. The
  counterexamples demonstrate failures of implication in that model. They do
  not establish the behavior of real grant or resource systems.
- The heartbeat results concern a specified map and progress projection, with
  the iteration theorem starting from idle. They are partial boundary examples
  for the cadence source card; no full cadence or liveness model is claimed.

CHARTER review separates integer Q from its natural-number bank specialization.
The source's positive-bank BPS application requires m>=1; the arithmetic
identities may also hold at m=0, where the smaller bank is zero. Gap-one
uniqueness is conditional on the given gap, while a separate positive-gap
lemma supplies the lower bound. Fixed-total Q minimization and the composite
subfamily are explicitly identified as derived consequences. Specific prime
checks do not establish a prime-valued sequence or any systems performance.

The Task-Keeper's original 8-12-target count objection remains in PLAN.md.

under the user's broader local-check grant. Counts are not scientific scores.

Open source obligations, inherited CHARTER core-inventory hardening, complete
PAL conformance, broader post-core claims, implementation, calibration, and
human adoption are outside the completion reached by this experiment.
