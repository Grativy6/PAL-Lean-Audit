## What the countercases can establish

The projection `(n,b) ↦ n`, with reconstruction `n ↦ (n,false)`, has exact capsule recovery and stable repeated normalization. It loses the distinction between `(n,false)` and `(n,true)`. That collision prevents full recovery from the capsule alone, and defeats the second-coordinate answer while preserving the first-coordinate answer. An additional retained side channel would change the model and reopen the claim.

This is a countermodel to inferring original recovery from capsule repeatability. It does not refute P1481: its work-roundtrip premise is false in this fixture. Conversely, exact work recovery does not require correct roundtrips on unreachable ambient capsules. The equations have different domains.

The fiber condition covers one specified `q : W → Q`; changing the question or continuation reopens the obligation. For the chosen thaw, the new theorem requires only `f(r(f(w))) = f(w)` on reachable capsules. Under this condition, preserving `q` is equivalent to fiber constancy. Fiber constancy alone establishes abstract reachable decodability, as checked in the earlier PAL batch, but does not validate a faulty thaw. Full work recovery is sufficient for every work-state answer, while a single coarser answer can survive a noninjective freeze map. There is no general abstract theorem here that all information must be lost; exact encodings are possible.

## Remaining burdens

These are function-level proofs, with hypotheses supplied explicitly. They do not verify a serializer, storage medium, complete mutation receipt, environment binding, deterministic program implementation, grant validation or token lineage. Those source burdens stay intact. A lossy answer-level transport profile would require its own declared domain, answer, adapter, residual, failure routing and adoption decision.
