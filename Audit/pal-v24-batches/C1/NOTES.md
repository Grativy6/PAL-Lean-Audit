This batch formalizes ordinary total functions, a reachable-image subtype, and
the finite Boolean-pair XOR fixture. It proves that the kernel of a paired
trace is the conjunction/intersection of its component kernels. It proves the
reachable decoder criterion; its reverse direction uses Classical.choice to
choose a preimage of each reachable value, and the axiom inspection records
that dependency.

The XOR fixture proves each coordinate insufficient while the paired trace is
sufficient. A separate constant-trace fixture has a conflicting answer pair
collapsed by both components and therefore blocks joint certification.

Limits: no finite-capacity cardinal bound, causal/cooperation/physical claim,
coalition claim, raw-capacity inference, ambient total decoder, computation
cost, or authority claim is formalized. This is a new source-bound C1 receipt
candidate; it does not relabel prior PAL interface coverage.
