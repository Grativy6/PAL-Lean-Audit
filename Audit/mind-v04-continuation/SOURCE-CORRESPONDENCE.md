## Relation to the PAL audit

The earlier PAL v2.3 interface, A2, A12, and A13 results motivate the questions; their historical receipts remain unchanged. The exact new statements live in `Experiments.MindContinuation`, and their controlling source references are MIND sections. They are not relabeled PAL obligations or conformance tests.

The key distinction from an abstract A12 equation is that this experiment names a concrete protected record and a particular resource-limited transition function. The completion theorem must expose the fuel and current-version conditions. A losing capsule must be tested against the specified continuation or protected query, not against an undefined notion of recovering everything.

No adopted PAL claim is weakened to fit the reduction. Non-work here means outside this experiment's protected-work equality. The rule, queued evidence, and receipt history are protected even though they include mechanics and bookkeeping. The full working state also includes consequential resources. Which fields belong in another task's protected projection must be declared separately.
