## Primitive-floor constraints

Attack Run 0002 must preserve all of the following:

1. Omega names unresolved possibility metalinguistically. No Lean type, object, value, constructor, carrier, set, model envelope, exterior, residue, bottom value, infinity, source, or semantic surrogate may be installed as Omega.
2. A0 is a supplied cut. It is not derived from Omega, M0, a certificate grammar, the absence of disproof, or later success.
3. Omega-shadow (the v2.0 Ω*/Omega-star reflection) is formed by and indexed to a particular cut. It is neither Omega nor A0 and does not reconstruct Omega.
4. Theta carries a predecessor-linked, trace-bearing turn or asymmetry. It selects no readable side and has no generic A1 eliminator.
5. A1 earns readable oriented distinction from a supplied readout witness and inherited orientation.
6. A2 earns a recoverable consequence or occurrence trace. It is not merely duration or continuous existence.
7. Residue is account-relative to a cut. What a cut excludes is not Omega.
8. Reopening preserves the earlier trace, certificates, costs, residuals, authority snapshot, and invalidation event. It returns to a current unresolved boundary and never resets to Omega.
9. No-free-distinction, trace conservation, witness-source honesty, no authority backflow, no branch absorption, typed authority, and local closure remain hard boundaries.
10. The repository T05 automation is a literal-identifier lexical guard only. Passing it is not a semantic proof that no differently named surrogate for unresolved possibility has been introduced; source review remains required.

## Candidate statements and audit routes

Each statement below is reproduced verbatim from the workflow and issue #4. The material after each quotation separates explicit PAL v2.0 support from the synthesis being proposed for testing.

### C21-01 — Account-relative primacy

**Status:** `CANDIDATE / PENDING_GATE_4`

> `A0` is the first addressable supplied cut in a declared account. It is not thereby the first event or distinction in all reality.

- **Explicit v2.0 route:** Spine SC-01 identifies A0 as the supplied cut and first asymmetry in the current PAL account; Ledger D03 identifies A0 as the first distinction admitted by PAL; Atlas M-A0-CUT requires a supplied witness and cost; T06 and the v2.0-C A0 migration card protect that identity.
- **Candidate synthesis:** “first addressable” and the express contrast with “all reality” are clarifications, not existing PAL identity sentences.
- **Assumptions for testing:** a declared account and scope; a supplied `CutReceipt`; an account ordering or admission index. “First” here is ledger/account order only.
- **Authority ceiling:** no temporal, causal, ontic, cosmological, or globally total primacy; no explanation of the witness's source.
- **Attack Run 0002 target:** `AR2-06 noGlobalFirstFromLocalFirst`.
- **Reopening handles:** SC-01 changed witness/scope/boundary/cost/admission basis; D03; M-A0-CUT; T06; O04/O25 for any source or absolute-first promotion.

### C21-02 — Cost-bearing cut

**Status:** `CANDIDATE / PENDING_GATE_4`

> Every addressable cut carries a cut-indexed cost or obligation. The cost may include exclusion, account-relative residue, witness burden, authority scope, and a route by which occurrence may later become recoverable.

- **Explicit v2.0 route:** Spine L01 requires the witness and cost for every admitted distinction; SC-01 and Atlas M-A0-CUT require a cost receipt on A0; the v2.0-C A0 migration card carries source and cost.
- **Candidate synthesis:** v2.0 does not make every listed receipt field a component of one cost. For this audit, `cost`, `exclusion`, `residue`, `witness`, `authority_ceiling`, and `reopening_or_recovery_route` remain separately typed fields. Any bridge among them must be explicit.
- **Assumptions for testing:** a supplied cut receipt with a cut-indexed cost value; the minimal algebra may reuse the ordered commutative-monoid pattern from M-A4-DEBT only as a new bounded A0 realization. M-A4-DEBT itself is an A4/SOOP transport card, not an existing A0 theorem.
- **Authority ceiling:** cost is ledger warrant only; it proves no cause, truth, physical energy, ultimate source, permission, or authority.
- **Attack Run 0002 target:** `AR2-04 costDoesNotImplyCause`, with a typed-field separation check.
- **Reopening handles:** L01; SC-01; M-A0-CUT; M-A4-DEBT for any proposed algebra; T06/T15; O04/O25 for any causal-source promotion.

### C21-03 — Occurrence-closed, source-open receipt

**Status:** `CANDIDATE / PENDING_GATE_4`

> A receipt may establish that a cut occurred, what it cost within the account, and what downstream consequences preserve it. The same receipt does not establish the cut's ultimate causal source or global primacy.

- **Explicit v2.0 route:** SC-01 and M-A0-CUT support local cut admission; SC-04 earns a readable A1 event; SC-05 and M-A2-STORE support recoverable occurrence/history; SC-18 and M-A15-CHECK support scoped closure; D14/O04/O25 keep ultimate source open; T11 and T16 preserve the A0/A1/A2 separation and trace.
- **Candidate synthesis:** “occurrence-closed, source-open” is not existing canonical language. A0 alone is insufficient: M-A0-CUT's ceiling includes no A1 occurrence. A positive occurrence instance requires a supplied A1 event/readout witness carried into an A2 recoverable trace. If the PAL status `CLOSED_IN_SCOPE` is emitted, an A15 closure receipt is also required. The source claim remains a separate `OPEN` field.
- **Assumptions for testing:** distinct A0, A1, A2, and optional A15 receipts with explicit predecessor links; a declared account, scope, protected fields, lookup/recovery route, tolerance where applicable, and supplied witnesses.
- **Authority ceiling:** local occurrence accounting and historical recoverability only; no continuous existence, ultimate causal source, source uniqueness, or global primacy.
- **Attack Run 0002 targets:** `AR2-03 occurrenceDoesNotIdentifySource` and `AR2-05 historicalPersistence`.
- **Reopening handles:** SC-04 witness/decoder/orientation changes; SC-05 collision/deletion/mutation/failed lookup; SC-18 dependency/scope/evidence/tolerance/authority changes; M-A2-STORE; M-A15-CHECK; O04/O25.

### C21-04 — Retrospective addressability

**Status:** `CANDIDATE / PENDING_GATE_4`

> A cut cannot witness its own primacy. Calling a cut "first" requires a later account with orientation, recoverable trace, and sufficient comparison structure.

- **Explicit v2.0 route:** SC-01/D03 already place A0 first in declared account/admission order without claiming temporal or ontic primacy. SC-03, SC-04, and SC-05 earn later orientation, readability, and recoverable trace separately. O04 negatively closes trace-only absolute-first identification.
- **Candidate synthesis:** for Attack Run 0002, “primacy” in the first sentence means temporal, ontic, causal, or global primacy—not the already-declared account order. If “comparison structure” invokes PAL's canonical A10, the route must also include SC-13, M-A10-PARTIAL, and O14. Otherwise the run must declare a bounded auxiliary comparator and must not borrow A10 authority.
- **Assumptions for testing:** an indexed account order; a later trace-bearing receipt for any historical claim; an explicitly scoped comparator for claims beyond mere account position.
- **Authority ceiling:** no global total order, no absolute-first inference, and no displacement of SC-01/D03's account order.
- **Attack Run 0002 target:** `AR2-06 noGlobalFirstFromLocalFirst`.
- **Reopening handles:** O04; SC-03/SC-04/SC-05; and, if used, SC-13/M-A10-PARTIAL/O14 whenever frame, map, domain, comparator, operation order, or tie band changes.

### C21-05 — Reflexive witness limit

**Status:** `CANDIDATE / PENDING_GATE_4`

> The act of inquiry witnesses the present occurrence of a distinction-making process. It does not prove the ontology, consciousness, ultimate source, or global priority of the inquirer or the addressed cut.

- **Explicit v2.0 route:** L05 and T15 forbid certificate self-invention; SC-04/SC-05 do not grant cause or consciousness; I05 and T17 forbid later authority backflow.
- **Candidate synthesis:** v2.0 does not state that inquiry self-certifies its occurrence. A formal positive instance is therefore conditional on a supplied, model-specific inquiry-event witness. The witness supports only a new local inquiry receipt.
- **Assumptions for testing:** an externally supplied or admitted inquiry-event witness; declared event identity, account, scope, cost, and trace route; no use of generated first-person output as privileged testimony.
- **Authority ceiling:** no ontology, consciousness, standing, consent, authority, ultimate source, original-cut witness, or global priority follows for the human, model, process, or addressed cut.
- **Attack Run 0002 target:** `AR2-07 inquiryCreatesNewReceipt`, restricted to a witnessed inquiry event; `AR2-03` supplies the source ceiling.
- **Reopening handles:** L05; T15; T17; the inquiry receipt's witness/scope/event identity; O04/O25 for any original-source claim.

### C21-06 — Cut-source underdetermination

**Status:** `CANDIDATE / PENDING_GATE_4`

> Identical accessible cuts do not warrant identification of identical upstream sources. A source claim requires a discriminating witness not already lost at the cut.

- **Explicit v2.0 route:** SC-01 leaves source and adequacy residual; M-A0-CUT does not derive source; D14/O04/O25 keep first-occurrence source open; L05/T15 forbid manufacture of the missing witness.
- **Candidate synthesis:** source fibers and downstream indistinguishability are new bounded mathematics. A noninjective countermodel shows that source injectivity is not derivable from the cut interface; it does not prove that every actual cut has several sources.
- **Assumptions for testing:** a declared map `sigma : Source -> Cut`; equality at the cut; downstream maps consume only `Cut`; unique-source results require an explicit injectivity assumption or separately supplied discriminating witness.
- **Authority ceiling:** no exhaustive source ontology, no identification of a fiber with Omega, no universal actual-world multiplicity claim, and no source uniqueness without an explicit bridge.
- **Attack Run 0002 targets:** `AR2-01 sourceFiberCountermodel` and `AR2-02 downstreamCannotDistinguishSameCut`.
- **Reopening handles:** SC-01 residual; M-A0-CUT; O04/O25; T15; any new discriminating witness, domain change, or injectivity assumption.

### C21-07 — Recursive source inquiry

**Status:** `CANDIDATE / PENDING_GATE_4`

> Asking what sourced a cut creates a further cut and incurs a further cost. The inquiry may reopen indefinitely without proving that reality itself contains an infinite causal regress.

- **Explicit v2.0 route:** multiple admitted cuts may be separately indexed; L01 requires witness and cost for each admitted distinction; L05 forbids witness manufacture; L08 and I09 make reopening append-only and history-preserving.
- **Candidate synthesis:** v2.0 does not say that every question automatically creates a cut. Attack Run 0002 models exactly one source-inquiry step only when a supplied inquiry-event witness and admission receipt are present. Iterated finite prefixes remain a candidate extension; no actually infinite object is tested.
- **Assumptions for testing:** an externally supplied inquiry-event witness; explicit admission of the inquiry as a new cut; the model scope, supplied cost, parent boundary identifier, and an exact retained prior field. No global freshness theorem is claimed.
- **Authority ceiling:** no proof of infinite causal regress, infinite ontology, inevitable recursion, or ultimate source; no claim that an unwitnessed question entered the ledger.
- **Attack Run 0002 target:** `AR2-07 inquiryCreatesNewReceipt`.
- **Reopening handles:** L01/L05/L08; I09; the new inquiry receipt's witness/cost/scope; O04/O25. This constructor retains one exact prior value; any recursive prefix theorem requires a separate induction and collision policy.

### C21-08 — No retroactive payment

**Status:** `CANDIDATE / PENDING_GATE_4`

> A later witness may recover a cost already incurred. It cannot create the earlier occurrence, manufacture its witness, or increase the earlier claim's authority.

- **Explicit v2.0 route:** I05/T17 prohibit authority backflow; L05/T15 prohibit witness manufacture; L04/T16 preserve protected trace; L08/I09 preserve earlier receipts through reopening; M-A2-STORE provides an append-only realization.
- **Candidate synthesis:** v2.0 does not define “retroactive payment.” In this audit, “recover” means recover or append an account of the already-recorded cost while preserving the earlier receipt byte-for-byte or field-for-field. The later receipt cannot alter the earlier occurrence, witness basis, cost, identity, uncertainty, or authority snapshot.
- **Assumptions for testing:** an earlier immutable receipt and cost snapshot; a later witness/result appended through a declared API; an exact prior-receipt projection or equality proof.
- **Authority ceiling:** no retrocausation, witness manufacture, cost erasure, authority increase, or rewriting of historical evidence.
- **Attack Run 0002 target:** `AR2-08 noRetroactivePayment`.
- **Reopening handles:** I05/I09; L04/L05/L08; M-A2-STORE; T15/T16/T17; any mismatch between the stored earlier receipt and its later projection.
