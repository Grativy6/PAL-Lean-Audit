# W00 journal 02 — Detection and generation are two jobs

2026-10-28. Mathematical findings and next tests; no claim of literature priority.

## Source-derived facts

CHARTER v1.0 §5 fixes A0=b(2a+b), A1=a²-b², A2=-a(a+2b). Its §7.2 six-line locus is their product's zero set. BRIDGE v0.4's positive adjacent family supplies F_lambda and R_lambda=F_lambda-N(lambda)^3 I; on the stated exceptional plane, F acts by lambda^6 and its conjugate. v0.6 §12 retains this development.

Claude's report already distinguishes a blind regime, a detecting-but-scalar regime, and a same-operator cyclic regime. The present work does not refute that table.

## Fresh derivation

Set B=3ab(a+b), T=(a-b)(2a+b)(a+2b), q=a²+ab+b². Then

- T²+3B²=4q³;
- the eta coefficient of lambda^6 is BT=-3A0A1A2;
- det(R_lambda)=3q³B²;
- det(w,F_lambda w)=BT*N(w), in the rational coordinate basis (1,eta).

Thus detection loses three lines; same-operator generation loses six. This difference should remain visible rather than being compressed into the slogan that one invariant controls both jobs identically.

## A second operator repairs the scalar case

Fix mu=2+eta, so mu^6=-323+360eta. For G=F_mu and u=R_lambda w,

    det(u, G u)=360*N(u)=1080*q³*B²*N(w).

For B!=0 and nonzero rational w this is nonzero even when T=0. Cramer's rule supplies explicit coordinates of any target in the pair (u,Gu).

Small exact control: lambda=1+eta has q=3, F=-27I, R=-54I. One seed and its F-image are dependent. The R-image of (1,0) and its G-image have determinant 1,049,760, so they reconstruct the plane.

## A tempting wrong shortcut caught

Do not use the action of eta itself as the auxiliary rotation: on this sixth exterior power eta^6=1, so that induced action is the identity. The fixed auxiliary parameter must have a nonreal sixth power. mu=2+eta does.

## Ideas retained for another key

1. Normal form: J=(G+143I)/180 satisfies J²=-3I. Every detector obeys 2R_lambda=-3B²I+BTJ. This makes the two jobs and the scalar failure transparent.
2. Minimal helper norm: prove, not merely search, that 7 is the smallest integral norm off the six lines. CHARTER's norm identity bounds the coordinates when q<7; this reduces the remaining check to a finite box.
3. With only a prescribed set of admissible correspondences, ask which generated rational operator algebra still supplies J. This is a concrete availability question, unlike assuming every endomorphism is free.
4. An actual algebraic-cycle construction remains a different investigation. Do not spend another evening tuning a scalar detector and report that as a cycle.
