# Framed Prime Shells v0.2 — manuscript source map

The complete working manuscript is stored in four ordered source parts so the v0.1 core remains visibly distinct from the v0.2 successor layer:

1. [`MANUSCRIPT_PART_00_FRONT.md`](MANUSCRIPT_PART_00_FRONT.md) — title, abstract, claim boundary, and coordinate floor.
2. [`MANUSCRIPT_PART_01_V01_CORE.md`](MANUSCRIPT_PART_01_V01_CORE.md) — classical shell boundary, local frame theorem, sharp constants, transport, and prime specialization.
3. [`MANUSCRIPT_PART_02_V02_ATLAS_RIBBONS.md`](MANUSCRIPT_PART_02_V02_ATLAS_RIBBONS.md) — derived versus independent axes, framed-shell atlas, trace-bearing ribbons, and three Smith charts.
4. [`MANUSCRIPT_PART_03_BACKMATTER.md`](MANUSCRIPT_PART_03_BACKMATTER.md) — formal receipt, literature boundary, nonclaims, conclusion, references, and disclosure.

The exact assembly order is the numbered order above. `assemble_manuscript.py` concatenates the parts without rewriting them.

The figure-bearing DOCX and PDF are publication-preparation artifacts generated from this source. The repository SVGs are readable review figures; final author geometry remains open at the seven marked insertion points.
